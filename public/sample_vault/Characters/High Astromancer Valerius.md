# High Astromancer Valerius

Valerius is the chief star-reader of [[Eldoria]], residing in the upper tower of the [[Arcane Observatory]].

## Background
Having served three generations of the royal family, Valerius maintains a strict neutrality in court politics. However, recent readings of the celestial spheres suggest an anomaly centered on the [[Obsidian Gate]].

## Personality & Relationship Links
- **Ally**: [[Queen Valerius]] (Advises the crown on arcane threats)
- **Rival**: [[Lord Malakor]] (Seeks to prevent Malakor from opening the [[Obsidian Gate]])
- **Location**: Works in the [[Arcane Observatory]] in [[Eldoria]]

- **Spell Save DC**: 16
